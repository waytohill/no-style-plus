Enable the table of contents by adding `toc: true` to front-matter. It auto-generates from all `## H2` headings in the post.

## How it works

`_includes/toc.html` parses the rendered HTML for `<h2 id="...">` elements emitted by kramdown's `auto_ids` feature (on by default). Each heading becomes a numbered item in the ToC.

H3 and deeper headings are not included — keeps the outline scannable.

## Anchor links

Every `##` and `###` heading gets an `#` anchor link (rendered by `anchor-links.js`). Hover the heading to reveal it. Clicking the `#` copies the full URL to the clipboard.

## Dark mode compatibility

The ToC box uses `border: thin solid black` directly — which inverts to white-on-dark correctly without any special handling.

## Long posts

For very long posts, the ToC helps readers navigate without scrolling. The `*:target` rule in the SCSS highlights the section jumped to in yellow.

## Summary

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| `toc: true` | Show auto-generated ToC |
| `toc: false` | No ToC (default) |
| H2 headings | Appear in ToC |
| H3+ headings | Not included in ToC |
