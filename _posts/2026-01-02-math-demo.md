Enable math rendering by setting `math: true` in front-matter. Formulas are rendered **server-side** by [jektex](https://github.com/yagarea/jektex) at build time — no client-side JS needed.

## Inline math

Use single dollar signs for inline math: $E = mc^2$, or $\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2} dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$.

You can also use named macros defined in `_config.yml`. For example: the rational numbers $\Q$ and the complex numbers $\C$.

## Display math

Use double dollar signs for display (block) math:

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}
$$

A more complex example:

$$
\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}
$$

## Adding macros

Custom macros live in `_config.yml` under `jektex.macros`:

```yaml
jektex:
  macros:
    - ["\\Q", "\\mathbb{Q}"]
    - ["\\C", "\\mathbb{C}"]
    - ["\\R", "\\mathbb{R}"]
```

## Alignment

$$
\begin{aligned}
(a+b)^2 &= a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \\
(a-b)^2 &= a^2 - 2ab + b^2
\end{aligned}
$$
